Sheaf Reader 束阅 · 隐私政策
Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-29

Sheaf Reader 束阅（以下简称「本应用」）是一个开源、离线的 RSS 阅读器。
一句话概括：本应用不设账号、不收集、不上传任何个人信息；你的订阅源、文章、
设置与凭据都只保存在你自己的设备上。

In one sentence: Sheaf Reader has no account system, collects nothing, and
uploads nothing. Your feeds, articles, settings and credentials stay on your
own device.

一、我们收集哪些信息
本应用不收集任何个人信息，也没有统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。
- 无账号、无登录、无手机号或邮箱收集。
- 无设备标识符、位置、通讯录、相册等信息的读取。
- 本应用仅申请一项权限：网络访问（ohos.permission.INTERNET），用于获取
  你自行添加的 RSS 订阅内容。

1. What we collect
Nothing. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
reporting SDK. The app requests a single permission, network access
(ohos.permission.INTERNET), which is used only to fetch the RSS feeds you add.

二、数据保存在哪里
- 订阅源、文章、已读 / 星标状态、界面设置均保存在本应用的本地沙箱内。
- 同步服务的服务器地址与 API 令牌保存在本机，仅用于向该服务发起鉴权请求。
- 备份文件由你主动导出到你选择的位置，内容为明文，请自行妥善保管。
- 卸载本应用或在系统设置中「清除数据」会删除上述本地数据。

2. Where data lives
Feeds, articles, read/starred state and preferences are stored in the app's
local sandbox. Sync server addresses and API tokens are stored on the device
and used only to authenticate against the service you configured yourself.
Backups are written in plain text to a location you pick. Uninstalling the app
(or clearing its data in system settings) deletes all of it.

三、网络请求发往何处
本应用只会连接以下两类地址，且都由你提供：
- 你添加的 RSS 订阅源地址；
- 你主动配置的同步服务（Miniflux、Google Reader、Inoreader、Feedbin、
  Nextcloud News、Fever）。
此外，为了让订阅列表显示缩略图，本应用会向你订阅源文章中引用的图片地址
发起请求。这些第三方服务器可能依据其自身隐私政策记录你的 IP 地址。本应用
不向上述任何一方附加设备标识或个人信息。

3. Where requests go
The app contacts only addresses you supply: the feed URLs you add, and the
sync service you configure. To show thumbnails it also requests images
referenced by your articles; those third-party servers may log your IP address
under their own policies. No device identifiers or personal data are attached
to any request.

四、第三方服务
本应用不集成第三方广告、统计或社交 SDK。当你点击文章中的链接时，内容会在
内置浏览器（系统 ArkWeb 组件）中打开；被访问网站的隐私实践由其自身决定，
不在本政策范围内。

4. Third-party services
No third-party advertising, analytics or social SDKs are bundled. Links you
open are shown in the built-in browser (the system ArkWeb component); the
privacy practices of those websites are their own and outside this policy.

五、儿童
本应用不面向 14 周岁以下儿童提供服务，也不会有针对性地收集儿童信息。

5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

六、你的权利
由于本应用不在服务器端保存任何数据，你无需申请导出或删除数据：卸载本应用
即可清除全部本地数据，你也可以随时在应用内删除单个订阅源、文章或清空缓存。

6. Your rights
Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing to export or delete
remotely: uninstalling clears all local data, and you can delete individual
feeds, articles or the cache inside the app at any time.

七、政策变更
本政策的更新会随应用版本一起发布，最新版本始终内置在应用中
（设置 → 关于 → 隐私政策）。

7. Changes
Updates ship inside the app; the current version is always available in
Settings → About → Privacy Policy.

八、联系我们
如对本政策有疑问，请在项目仓库提交 Issue：
https://github.com/simon2137/Sheaf-Reader/issues

8. Contact
Questions about this policy: open an issue at
